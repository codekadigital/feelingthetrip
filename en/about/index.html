Baja California Sur · 24°08'N 110°18'W

How it works

Some places wake that need to explore, to keep walking without knowing what lies behind the next mountain. We go, we film it, we tell it.

When a place deserves it, we come back with people. Very small groups, because a large group changes the place it arrives at and a small one adapts to it.

The rest of the year we edit, answer emails and plan the next one. We are two people, not an agency.
