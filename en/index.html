Descend

The manifesto

Not only the sea. Everything that breathes.

Some moments cannot be planned: they simply happen. A skipper shouting "get ready, now!", a flat calm sea, and suddenly something enormous moving underneath.

And then there is the other half. It was never going to be only sea, sand and salt in your hair: we also love landscapes of fog, wind and rain. A cliff in the Faroes demands exactly the attention a cenote does twenty metres down.

Behind every photograph there are early starts, kilometres walked and conditions that rarely cooperate. That is the part you do not see.

The two of us

Alba & Miguel

Alba

The mind behind every adventure

Born in Alicante and a traveller by nature. I studied Tourism and Business Management — perhaps because travelling was always going to be part of my path. I am the dreamer and the organiser of the team: I love imagining destinations, designing routes and turning an idea into an unforgettable trip. I enjoy living the adventure as much as preparing every detail so others can live it too.

Miguel

The eye behind the camera

From Madrid, an engineer, and almost always with a camera in his hands. I am the technical and creative side of Feeling the Trip. I love capturing the moments that are hard to put into words: perfect light, a landscape that leaves you speechless, that feeling of living something you know you will always remember. Through film and photography, I try to turn every trip into a story.
